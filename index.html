What ConeView does

A field tool for security installers, integrators and consultants. ConeView plans installs; it does not connect to your cameras or NVR.

CCTV lens calculator

Field of view, scene width, pixel density and DORI distances from the focal length and sensor, plus mount height and tilt. Lens Preview shows roughly what a lens will frame through your iPhone camera.

Site survey

Scan rooms with LiDAR and merge them into one floor plan, or import a PDF or image plan and set its scale with two taps. Add pillars, racks and walls the scan missed.

Coverage and blind spots

Place cameras on the plan or in AR. Coverage is traced around walls and obstacles, shaded by DORI level, and blind spots show in red.

Branded proposals

A PDF with your logo: the floor plan with coverage, a camera schedule with mount photos, a parts list by lens, assumptions and the disclaimer.

Which devices work

Every iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later

The lens calculator, Lens Preview, the PoE calculator, imported floor plans, camera placement, coverage, the camera schedule and PDF proposals.

Room scanning needs LiDAR

  • iPhone: iPhone 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max and every later Pro and Pro Max model.
  • iPad: iPad Pro 11-inch (2nd generation) and iPad Pro 12.9-inch (4th generation) and later iPad Pro models.

ConeView checks your device during setup. Without LiDAR, import a PDF or image floor plan instead.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ConeView cost?

All of ConeView’s tools come with ConeView Pro: $13.99 per week or $130.99 per year (US prices; the App Store shows the price for your country). Eligible new subscribers start with a 7-day free trial, one per Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ConeView and tap Cancel Subscription. In ConeView you can also go to Settings > Subscription > Manage subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the Apple Account you subscribed with, open ConeView and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or go to Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases. You don’t need an account with us.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does Scan with LiDAR say “Needs LiDAR”?

Room scanning needs a LiDAR sensor (see “Which devices work” above). On other devices, choose Import plan (PDF or image) right below it and set the scale by marking two points a known distance apart. Everything else works the same.

How accurate is the coverage?

Coverage results are planning estimates based on lens geometry and the scanned or imported plan. Always verify on site before installing. DORI levels use the IEC 62676-4 pixel densities.

Does ConeView connect to my cameras or NVR?

No. ConeView is a planning tool. It does not stream, record or control cameras.

Where are my projects stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and no ConeView server. Your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) includes your projects, so they come back when you restore a backup to a new device. Deleting ConeView deletes them. Read the privacy policy.
