Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD

What you enter in ConeView stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Apart from the App Store, ConeView talks only to RevenueCat, which records subscriptions for us: each time you open ConeView it checks in under a random, anonymous ID, and it sends your App Store subscription history when you start a free trial, subscribe or restore purchases, so we can count trials, renewals and cancellations.

Who we are

ConeView is published on the App Store by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD (“we”). This policy covers the ConeView app for iPhone and iPad.

What stays on your device

Your projects, floor plans (scanned or imported), camera placements, saved calculations, PoE worksheets, mount photos, company branding (logo, company name, phone, email and website), your answers to the welcome questions (including your first name, if you give it), copies of the proposals you share, and your app settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Deleting ConeView deletes this data. Your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include it, under the settings you chose for those backups.

Camera and room scanning

Lens Preview uses the camera to show the live image with a crop frame for the lens you picked. ConeView doesn’t record, save or send anything Lens Preview shows.

When you scan a room, the camera and LiDAR sensor build a 3D model on your device. ConeView keeps the model’s geometry — walls, openings, floors and obstacles — so you can plan on it. Camera pictures aren’t kept.

When you place cameras in AR, the camera shows the live view of the room on your device so you can aim each camera. ConeView doesn’t record, save or send anything it shows.

Mount photos you take or choose are stored only in ConeView on your device. They aren’t added to your photo library, and location and other metadata is stripped before storing. Nothing is uploaded.

Photos

When you add a company logo, a mount photo or a floor plan, the system photo picker or the Files browser gives ConeView only the image or document you choose. ConeView keeps a copy on your device for your branding, camera records or plan and doesn’t access the rest of your library. Location and other metadata is removed from floor-plan images before they are stored, and a plan PDF keeps only the page you pick, without its document information (author, title and similar).

If you choose Save Image when you share a plan image, iOS asks your permission and adds that image to your photo library. ConeView only adds the image you chose and never reads your library.

Proposals and sharing

Proposal PDFs and floor-plan images are created on your device from your projects. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, through the share sheet, to a place you choose. ConeView keeps a copy of the latest proposal and plan image you shared with each project on your device.

Subscriptions

Purchases and free trials are handled by Apple through the App Store. ConeView receives your subscription status from the App Store so it can unlock Pro. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Subscription analytics

ConeView uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our processor to record subscription events. RevenueCat creates a random, anonymous ID on your device. Each time you open ConeView or switch back to it, whether or not you have subscribed, ConeView checks in with RevenueCat under that ID: it asks for your subscription record (RevenueCat creates an empty one the first time) and for the app’s subscription settings, and it looks on your device for ConeView App Store purchases that RevenueCat hasn’t recorded yet and sends any it finds. When you start a free trial, subscribe or restore purchases, and when the App Store reports a renewal, cancellation or refund, RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase history for ConeView (the plan, its price and currency, the dates, and the trial and renewal status) under the same ID.

Like any request an app makes to a server, each check-in and each purchase record also carries technical details: the app and iOS version, the device model, your language and App Store country, the identifier iOS gives apps from the same developer on your device (not the advertising identifier), and your IP address. ConeView never sends RevenueCat your name, email address, location, projects, plans or photos.

We use this only to record trials, renewals and cancellations and to see how many people subscribe. It isn’t linked to your identity and isn’t used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Apart from the subscription records above, ConeView contains no analytics, advertising or attribution software and doesn’t track you across apps or websites.

Contacting support

If you email us at support@qidientlabs.com, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply to you.

Children

ConeView is a tool for security installers and isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and shows a new effective date here. For questions, email support@qidientlabs.com.
